What is SupreCart?

A trusted marketplace built for UNILORIN students. Sellers come from Tanke, Oke-Odo, Sanrab and inside campus, so you know who you're dealing with. Everything — listing, chat, payment, handoff and payout — happens in one app.

Project at a glance

Campus commerce, end to end

Students open a shop, list items with photos, chat with buyers, take protected payments and get paid to their bank. Riders handle delivery inside the 2-hour promise, while staff keep the market fair.

  • Categories: thrift, wigs, sneakers, food, gadgets and more.
  • Payments: Paystack — money is held until the buyer confirms receipt.
  • Realtime: in-app chat between buyer and seller on every order.
  • Trust: verified email, reviews, moderation and an admin back office.

How it works

The same money rules on both sides: payment is protected until the buyer confirms, then the seller gets paid.

🛒

Buying an item

  1. Create your account and confirm your email.
  2. Find what you need — search or browse categories.
  3. Chat with the seller to agree price and meetup or delivery.
  4. Pay in the app. Your money is held until you get the item.
  5. Confirm receipt to complete the order and release payment.
💸

Selling an item

  1. Open your shop and verify your email.
  2. Post the item — photos, title, condition, price.
  3. Answer buyers in chat and accept the order.
  4. Hand over the item (meetup or rider pickup).
  5. Get paid — payout lands in your bank account.

Step-by-step guides

Screenshot walkthroughs for the three things students do most. Use Previous / Next on each strip.

Guide 1 · List a product

From opening the app to a live listing other students can buy.

Step 1: Start your listing

  1. Open SupreCart and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap Sell or List item.
  3. Choose the right category and add clear photos.

Step 2: Add details and price

  1. Enter a clear title and honest description.
  2. Set condition and anything buyers should know.
  3. Choose your price and pickup or delivery info.

Step 3: Publish and sell

  1. Review photos, details and price.
  2. Tap Publish to make it visible on campus.
  3. Reply to buyers, hand off, and get paid.

Guide 2 · Buy and pay safely

Search → chat → protected payment → confirm receipt.

Step 1: Find it

  1. Open the app and search or browse categories.
  2. Open the listing and check photos, price and seller.
  3. Tap Buy or message the seller first.

Step 2: Pay with protection

  1. Confirm delivery option (meetup or rider).
  2. Pay with card, transfer or USSD via Paystack.
  3. Your money is held — not released yet.

Step 3: Confirm receipt

  1. Meet the seller or wait for the rider.
  2. Check the item, then tap Confirm receipt.
  3. Payment releases — leave a review.

Guide 3 · Sell and get paid

Shop setup → orders → handoff → bank payout.

Step 1: Open your shop

  1. Verify your email first (required to sell).
  2. Add your shop name and short bio.
  3. Add bank details in settings for payouts.

Step 2: Take the order

  1. Buyer pays — you see a new order.
  2. Chat to agree handoff time and place.
  3. Mark ready / handed over when you give the item.

Step 3: Get paid

  1. Buyer confirms receipt → payment releases.
  2. Payout is sent to your bank account.
  3. Track every naira on the Payouts page.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes — free to download and use. A small commission applies when an item is sold.

How do payments stay safe?

Paystack collects the payment and SupreCart holds it until you confirm you received the item. Only then is the seller paid.

How fast is delivery?

Campus deliveries target within 2 hours. You can also arrange a direct meetup with the seller in chat.

Who can sell?

Verified UNILORIN students. Confirm your email, open a shop, add bank details, and you're live.

Will it come to the Play Store?

That's planned. Once it's live you'll be able to update from there.
