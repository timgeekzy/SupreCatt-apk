Install in four steps

SupreCart is shared as an APK file, so Android asks for one extra permission the first time.

  1. Download the fileTap the download button. If your browser warns that the file could be harmful, choose to keep it.
  2. Open itTap the downloaded SupreCart file from your notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the installIf asked, turn on “Allow from this source” for your browser, then go back.
  4. Tap Install and open the appCreate your account and start browsing.

Questions

Why does Android show a warning?

Android warns about every app installed outside the Play Store. It doesn’t mean something is wrong. Only download SupreCart from this page or from a link shared by the SupreCart team.

How can I check the file is genuine?

Compare the file’s SHA-256 checksum with this one: Checksum coming soon

Will it work on my phone?

This version is built for 64-bit Android phones, which covers most phones made in the last several years. If Android says the app can’t be installed, your phone may be too old for this build.

Is it free?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. A small commission applies when an item is sold.

Will it come to the Play Store?

That’s planned. Once it’s live you’ll be able to update from there.
