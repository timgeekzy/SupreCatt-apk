How to list a product

Follow these steps in the app to create your listing and start selling on campus.

Step 1: Start your listing

  1. Open SupreCart and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Sell or List item option.
  3. Choose the right category and add clear photos of your item.

Step 2: Add details and price

  1. Enter a clear title and honest description.
  2. Set the item condition and any important details buyers should know.
  3. Choose your price and confirm your pickup or delivery info.

Step 3: Publish and sell

  1. Review your photos, details, and price for accuracy.
  2. Tap Publish to make your listing visible to students.
  3. Respond to buyers, complete the handoff, and get paid safely.

Install in four steps

SupreCart is shared as an APK file, so Android asks for one extra permission the first time.

  1. Download the fileTap the download button. If your browser warns that the file could be harmful, choose to keep it.
  2. Open itTap the downloaded SupreCart file from your notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the installIf asked, turn on “Allow from this source” for your browser, then go back.
  4. Tap Install and open the appCreate your account and start browsing.

How to use the app

Buy from students around you, or turn what you no longer need into cash. Here’s the flow either way.

🛍️

Buying an item

  1. Create your account and open the app to see listings from your campus.
  2. Find what you need by searching or browsing the categories.
  3. Pay in the app. Your payment is held until you get the item, so you don’t have to pay and hope.
  4. Confirm once you have it to complete the order and release the payment.
💸

Selling an item

  1. Post your item with clear photos, a title, your price and a short description.
  2. Chat with buyers and agree on the price and a safe place to meet or deliver.
  3. Hand over the item and get paid straight to your bank.

Questions

Why does Android show a warning?

Android warns about every app installed outside the Play Store. It doesn’t mean something is wrong. Only download SupreCart from this page or from a link shared by the SupreCart team.

How can I check the file is genuine?

Will it work on my phone?

This version is built for 64-bit Android phones, which covers most phones made in the last several years. If Android says the app can’t be installed, your phone may be too old for this build.

Is it free?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. A small commission applies when an item is sold.

That’s planned. Once it’s live you’ll be able to update from there.
